# PinDesk Privacy Policy

[← Back to the English overview](README.md) · [Español](../docs/privacidad.md)

This policy explains what data the **PinDesk** app (for Windows, Android, Mac and iPad, including
**PinDesk Templates** / *PinDesk Plantillas*) handles, why, and what you can do about it. The Spanish
version is the reference text; if the two differ, the Spanish one prevails.

Effective date: November 7, 2026. Last updated: November 7, 2026.

> **In short**
>
> - What you create in PinDesk is stored **on your device**.
> - You sign in to PinDesk with **Google**. From your account we use your **email address** and your
>   **account ID**, only for your plan, your devices and sync.
> - **Payments are processed by payment providers.** We never see or store your card details.
> - With **Pro**, what you choose to sync through the cloud is stored on our servers, encrypted in
>   transit.
> - What you ask the **AI** is sent to the AI provider you choose.
> - **No ads, no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking.** We do not sell data.
> - **Wi‑Fi** sync goes encrypted directly between your devices, without going through the
>   internet.
> - Speech-to-text and reading text in images run **on your device**.

## 1. Who is responsible

PinDesk is developed by **Max Anthony Mamani Maquera**, an independent developer based in Peru
("we"). He is the controller of the data described in sections 2, 5 and 6. For any privacy matter,
write to **max.antony.8.5.1@gmail.com**.

## 2. Your Google account

PinDesk requires **signing in with Google**. From your account we receive and store:

- your Google **email address**;
- your **Google account ID** (a number Google assigns to each account).

We use them to:

- know which **plan** you have (Free or Pro) and link any licence you buy to your account;
- keep the **list of your devices** (for Pro's 3-device limit). For each device we store an
  identifier generated by PinDesk, its name, its operating system and when it last connected;
- **sync your data through the cloud** between your devices, if you have Pro;
- **reply to you** if you contact us about your account or a payment.

PinDesk checks with our servers from time to time to confirm the status of your account and
licence. Nothing you create is sent in that check.

**Signing in with Google does not give us access to your Drive, Calendar or email.** That only
happens if you connect those services to the assistant yourself (section 4), and it is asked for
separately.

Legal basis: performance of the contract you accept by using PinDesk (the [Terms](terms.md)).

## 3. Data kept on your device

PinDesk stores in its data folder (on Windows, `%APPDATA%\PinDesk`, or next to the program in the
portable version; on Android, Mac and iPad, in the app's private storage) what you create or bring
into it:

- Chats, messages, notes, tasks, timetables, reminders, lessons, templates and documents.
- Screenshots, pins, drawings, photos, videos, voice notes and class or meeting recordings.
- Transcripts, topics and notes from a meeting. **Speech is turned into text on your device**, with
  models that run on it or with the system's speech recogniser.
- Text recognition in images (OCR) and reading PDF, Word, Excel and drawings also happen on your
  device.
- Your settings.
- Your Google **session** and any **API keys** you paste (for example TypeSafe or Brave Search):
  they are stored **encrypted** with the system's protections (on Windows, with your Windows user)
  and never in the settings file.
- Reports you make about AI responses (section 4).

We have no access to this data unless it leaves your device through one of the ways in sections 4
and 6.

## 4. Data that leaves your device

Besides sections 2, 5 and 6, something leaves your device **only when you use a feature that needs
it**. None of these features turns itself on. Everything travels over an encrypted connection
(HTTPS/TLS), and each service handles data under its own privacy policy. On Windows, **Settings →
General → Privacy and data** shows this same list, each with a button to where it is turned on or
off.

| Feature | What is sent | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| **AI assistant** ⭐ and **Templates with AI** ⭐ | What you write, the recent conversation and what you attach (a photo, a screenshot, a document's text). | The AI provider you choose in Settings → Assistant: **Anthropic** (Claude) or **DeepSeek**. |
| **Transcript summaries** ⭐ | The transcript text (never the audio). | The AI provider you choose. |
| **Jev** ⭐ (task urgency and meeting topics) | Text snippets (sentences already transcribed on your device, note lines, tasks) and, in some features, an image, to get a classification. **Never the audio.** | **TypeSafe**, only with that feature turned on. |
| **Assistant web search** ⭐ | Search terms and the addresses of the pages it reads. No cookies or account data. | DuckDuckGo, or Brave Search with your Brave key; and the pages read. |
| **Google connections** | The Drive files you pick and the calendar events you ask for. | Google, only if you connect those services. Disconnect them in Settings → Assistant. |
| **Reader's online voices** | The text read aloud. | Microsoft, only if you pick one of its online voices; system voices work offline. |
| **Share by link** | The file you choose to share. | A public temporary file host. Anyone with the link can open it until it expires (hours to days). Do not use it for private things. |
| **Model and dictionary downloads** | Only the download request (the server sees your IP address). Nothing of yours. | The sites that publish those models (for example Hugging Face or GitHub). |
| **Links you open** | Handled by your browser or the program you choose. | The destination site or program. |

⭐ = PinDesk Pro feature.

**About AI requests.** We do not use the content of your AI requests for anything other than giving
you the answer, and we do not store it on our servers. The AI provider handles it under its own
terms: review them before sending it sensitive information.

**AI-generated content.** Responses come from third-party models and may be inaccurate. You can
report an inappropriate response with **Report response…** (message menu › More): pick a reason, it
is saved in your data folder and your email program opens with the report written, so you can send
it to us if you wish. We only receive what you choose to send.

## 5. Payments

If you buy **PinDesk Pro**, the payment is processed by a **payment provider** (for example the card
gateway, PayPal or Yape, depending on the method you choose). Your payment details (card number,
PayPal or Yape account) **are received and handled by that provider, not by us**. **We do not store
your card details.**

From the payment provider we receive and keep only what is needed to activate your licence, support
you and meet our legal and accounting obligations: your account email, the plan bought, amount,
currency, date, billing country, payment status (paid, renewed, cancelled or refunded) and the
transaction ID.

Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract and compliance with legal obligations.

## 6. Sync

### Over Wi‑Fi (all plans)

Your devices find each other on the local network and pair with a code or a QR code. Everything that
travels is **encrypted** with a key only your devices know, directly from one to the other, **with
no intermediate servers on the internet**. If you share a chat with someone, that person receives a
copy on their device.

### Through the cloud (Pro)

If you have **Pro** and use cloud sync, the chats, files and changes you sync are uploaded to **our
servers** (hosted by a cloud provider we contract) so they reach your other devices, and the people
in any **cloud shared chats** you belong to. They travel **encrypted** (HTTPS/TLS) and only changes
are uploaded.

We use that data **only to sync it**: we do not look at it, analyse it or use it for anything else.
We would only access it if you ask us to, to solve a problem, or if a law or competent authority
requires it.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (the sync service you get with Pro).

## 7. What we don't do

- No ads and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, remote crash reports or trackers. App logs stay on your device.
- We do not sell, rent or give away personal data.
- We do not use your data to train AI models. (The AI providers you send data to have their own
  terms; review them.)

## 8. Retention and deletion

- **On your device**, data is kept until you delete it. On Windows, **Settings → General → Your
  data, on your disk** opens the data folder: copy it to take your data with you, or close PinDesk
  and delete it to remove everything. Uninstalling may leave that folder (so nothing is lost by
  accident); delete it by hand if you wish.
- **Your account and device list** are kept while you use PinDesk. You can ask us to delete your
  account at any time.
- **Cloud data** is kept while you have an account. If Pro lapses, **we do not delete it for that
  reason**: cloud sync pauses and catches up when you renew. You can ask us to delete it whenever you
  want, and we delete it if you delete your account. Backups on our servers are overwritten within
  up to 30 days.
- **The payment data** we keep (section 5) is kept for the time tax and accounting law requires,
  even if you delete your account.
- **What you sent to a third party** (section 4) is governed by that service. Temporary links expire
  on their own.
- **Copies on your other devices**, or on the devices of people you shared a chat with, are deleted
  on those devices.

## 9. Permissions

Microphone (voice notes, dictation, recording classes or meetings), screen capture and camera
(photos, clips, scanning a QR code) are used only for features you start. Network access is used to
sign in, check your licence, for the features in section 4 and for sync. Notifications are used for
task, class and exam reminders. Start with the system only if you turn it on. PinDesk does not read
your contacts, location or email.

## 10. Security

Your session and keys are stored encrypted on your device. Internet connections use HTTPS and Wi‑Fi
sync is encrypted between your devices. Access to our servers is limited to what is essential. No
system is infallible: protect your device and your Google account with a password and, if you can,
two-step verification. If a security incident affects your data, we will tell you and notify the
authority where the law requires.

## 11. Children

PinDesk is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age of consent in your country) and
does not knowingly collect children's data. Children under that age may only use PinDesk with the
permission and supervision of their parents or guardians. AI features use third-party services that
may require a higher minimum age. If you believe a child has given us data without that permission,
write to us and we will delete it.

## 12. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example Peru's **Personal Data Protection Law
No. 29733** and its regulations, the EU **GDPR** or Spain's LOPDGDD), you have the right to
**access**, **rectify**, **cancel or erase** your data, **object** to its processing and, where
applicable, to **portability**. You control the data on your device directly. For your account,
cloud or payment data, write to **max.antony.8.5.1@gmail.com** from your account email; we will
reply within the time limits set by law.

You can also complain to your data protection authority: in Peru, the **National Authority for
Personal Data Protection** (Ministry of Justice and Human Rights); in Spain, the AEPD.

## 13. International transfers

Our servers, payment providers and AI providers may be outside your country (for example in the
United States, the European Union or, if you choose DeepSeek, China). When you use those features,
your data may be processed there. We choose providers that offer adequate protection safeguards.

## 14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version at this same address with its date. If
the change is important, we will also announce it in the app before it applies.
